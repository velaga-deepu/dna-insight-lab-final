3D DNA helix

Type a DNA sequence and see it built as a double helix. Drag the helix to turn it.

  • A, adenine (pairs with T)
  • T, thymine (pairs with A)
  • G, guanine (pairs with C)
  • C, cytosine (pairs with G)
  • Teal backbone: your sequence
  • Violet backbone: its complement

This is an idealised B-DNA model: right-handed, 10.5 base pairs per turn. A real molecule's exact shape is measured in a lab, so treat this as a model, not a photograph.
